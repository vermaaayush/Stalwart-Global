

STALWART GROUP

Building Global Trade. Creating Stronger Markets.

Stalwart Group is a diversified international business group engaged in sourcing, trade, supply chain management, logistics, distribution, hospitality and market development across multiple industries.

Through our specialized businesses — Stalwart Global, Stalwart Resources and Stalwart Life Sciences — we operate across hospitality, FMCG, consumer goods, coffee, agricultural commodities, food ingredients, livestock nutrition and wellness.

With a business network spanning India, China, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Cambodia, Thailand and Australia, we connect reliable producers, manufacturers and suppliers with businesses and consumers across growing international markets.

From identifying the right source to moving products across borders, building distribution networks and operating consumer-facing businesses, Stalwart Group works across the complete commercial value chain.

INDIAN TADKA

Bringing Indian Hospitality to International Markets

A major part of Stalwart Global’s hospitality portfolio is Indian Tadka, the group’s flagship Indian restaurant brand.

Indian Tadka has grown into a leading Indian restaurant chain in Myanmar, with operations across Myanmar and Sri Lanka.

Built around authentic Indian flavours, consistent quality and welcoming hospitality, Indian Tadka serves a diverse customer base including local diners, families, expatriates, travellers and corporate customers.

Its expansion across international markets demonstrates Stalwart Group’s ability not only to trade and distribute products, but also to build, operate and scale consumer-facing businesses across borders.
