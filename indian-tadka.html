Bringing Indian Hospitality to International Markets

A major part of Stalwart Global’s hospitality portfolio is Indian Tadka, the group’s flagship Indian restaurant brand.

Indian Tadka has grown into a leading Indian restaurant chain in Myanmar, with operations expanding across Myanmar and Sri Lanka. Built around authentic Indian flavours, consistent quality and welcoming hospitality, Indian Tadka serves local diners, families, expatriates, travellers and corporate clients.
