Flagship Hospitality Brand

INDIAN TADKA

The flagship culinary hospitality brand of Stalwart Global, operating premier dining establishments across Myanmar and Sri Lanka with uncompromised culinary authenticity and service excellence.

Consumer Excellence

From Sourcing Spices to Serving Experiences.

Indian Tadka has established itself as the premier destination for authentic Indian culinary heritage in Myanmar and Sri Lanka.

Built around authentic regional Indian recipes, uncompromising ingredient quality, and warm, attentive hospitality, Indian Tadka welcomes a diverse and discerning clientele — from local epicures and families to international expatriates, diplomats, and corporate leaders.

Its international growth demonstrates Stalwart Group's unique capability: not only trading agricultural commodities and wholesale products, but building, operating, staffing, and scaling customer-facing retail brands in competitive international markets.

Culinary Integrity

Authentic Heritage. Tandoori Mastery.

Every dish is crafted from origin-sourced Indian whole spices, premium basmati rice, and slow-cooked tandoori marinades.

Our executive chefs bring decades of royal Indian culinary tradition, balancing complex aromatics with contemporary international plating. By combining traditional clay tandoors with modern food safety protocols, Indian Tadka delivers an unforgettable dining experience across every location.
