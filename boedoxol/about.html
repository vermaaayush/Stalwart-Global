5+ Years Experience

I'm Alex — a freelance motion graphics and graphic designer with over 5 years of experience crafting visual stories that captivate audiences. From brand identities to cinematic motion pieces, I blend creativity with strategy to deliver designs that don't just look good — they work .

My approach is rooted in understanding the story behind every brand. Whether it's a startup looking to make their first impression or an established company refreshing their visual language, I bring the same level of passion and precision to every project.

I specialize in motion graphics, branding & identity, UI/UX design, and creative direction — helping businesses transform ideas into compelling visual experiences that resonate with their audience and drive results.
