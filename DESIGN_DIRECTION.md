# STALWART GROUP — DESIGN DIRECTION (Client-Approved)

## Visual Identity — Core Rules

### Theme
- **Full dark/black theme** across the entire website
- Near-black (`#050505`) as the primary background
- Dark architectural panels, charcoal, graphite tones for layering
- **NO light sections, NO white backgrounds**

---

### Text Colors — STRICT RULE
Only **white and grey** tones for all text:

| Usage | Color | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary headlines | White with grey shadow/shade effect | Chronicle/cinematic style — large text with subtle grey-to-white gradient or shadow giving depth |
| Section titles | Off-white `#E8E8E8` | Clean, readable |
| Body copy | Muted grey `#999999` – `#AAAAAA` | Understated, elegant |
| Labels / metadata | Muted grey `#666666` – `#777777` | Small uppercase, technical |
| Hover / active states | Pure white `#FFFFFF` | Subtle lift |

**NO colored text.** No blue links, no green highlights, no colored accents in text.

---

### Gold — ONLY for the Lion Logo
- **Gold is reserved exclusively for the Lion Shield logo** — the core company identity
- The lion logo is the **single golden element** on the site
- Gold tone: Metallic gold as seen in the approved hero mockup
- Gold hex range: `#C5A55A` → `#D4AF37` → `#B8942E` (metallic gradient)
- Minimal gold accents allowed: navigation active indicator (thin line), section counter current number, explore arrow border — but **extremely restrained**
- Gold must never dominate. The lion is the star.

---

### The Lion Shield Logo
- **Core brand identity of Stalwart Group**
- Metallic gold lion head inside a heraldic shield
- Lion faces right, majestic with flowing mane
- Used in: Hero (centered, large), Header (small wordmark companion), Footer, Favicon
- Logo file saved: `assets/images/stalwart-lion-logo-gold.jpg`
- **Must always appear in gold against dark backgrounds**
- Never flatten to white/grey — the gold lion IS the brand

---

### Typography Style — "Chronicle" / Cinematic
- Headlines use an **elegant serif or display typeface** with a cinematic feel
- Text should have a **shade effect** — subtle gradient from white to grey, or a slight drop shadow that gives dimensional depth
- Think: movie title cards, luxury annual reports, architectural signage
- Very large headlines with wide letter-spacing
- Strong contrast between massive display type and tiny metadata labels
- The word "STALWART" in the hero is oversized, subtly shaded grey with depth — this style must carry through the site

---

### Background Treatment
- Near-black with subtle **architectural panel textures**
- Vertical dark panels with slight tonal variation (as seen in hero)
- Subtle dark-on-dark texture (stone, slate, concrete feel)
- **No flat solid black** — use layers of dark tones for depth
- Fine 1px borders in dark grey (`#1A1A1A` – `#222222`) for section separators

---

### UI Elements
- Buttons: Ghost/outline style with white/grey borders, white text
- Borders: Thin 1px, dark grey
- Separators: Fine horizontal lines
- Navigation: White text, gold active indicator (thin underline)
- Section numbers: Small, muted grey
- Scroll indicators: Minimal, white/grey
- Explore CTA: Circular outline with arrow (as in hero)

---

## Summary — The Visual Equation

```
DARK BLACK THEME
+ WHITE/GREY CHRONICLE TEXT WITH SHADING
+ GOLD LION LOGO (ONLY GOLD ELEMENT)
+ ARCHITECTURAL DARK PANELS
+ CINEMATIC EDITORIAL TYPOGRAPHY
+ FINE-LINE MINIMAL UI
= STALWART GROUP WEBSITE
```
