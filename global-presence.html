Strategic Footprint

GLOBAL PRESENCE

Our international footprint spans active sourcing networks, operating hubs, and trade corridors across India, China, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Cambodia, Thailand, and Australia.

Key Regional Markets

Seven Strategic Hubs

Our presence across primary Asian manufacturing hubs and dynamic consumer markets enables us to remain closer to suppliers, customer preferences, and emerging opportunities.

01
INDIA — HQ SOURCING & TRADE

A cornerstone market for origin agricultural sourcing, spices, pulses, rice, life sciences formulation, institutional hospitality procurement, and global corporate business development.

02
CHINA — MANUFACTURING HUB

A primary manufacturing and volume sourcing powerhouse supporting consumer goods, industrial equipment, and international supply chain procurement.

03
MYANMAR — HOSPITALITY & DISTRIBUTION

A flagship commercial and retail operating market, home to multi-city Indian Tadka restaurants and regional wholesale distribution channels.

04
SRI LANKA — COMMERCIAL EXPANSION

An active commercial and hospitality center featuring Indian Tadka dining, spice sourcing corridors, and strategic island trade initiatives.

05
CAMBODIA — ASEAN EXPANSION

A fast-emerging node in Stalwart's Southeast Asian agricultural sourcing network, livestock nutrition, and regional trade partnerships.

06
THAILAND — REGIONAL CORRIDOR

An indispensable regional crossroads supporting international trade logistics, sugar trade flows, food processing ingredients, and hospitality sourcing.

07
AUSTRALIA — OCEANIA CORRIDOR

A premier trading and strategic partner market driving bilateral agricultural commodity flow, specialty coffee development, and life sciences collaboration.
