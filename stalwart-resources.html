Connecting Origins With Markets.

Stalwart Resources is the agricultural commodities, coffee and food ingredients division of Stalwart Group.

We specialize in the sourcing, trading and distribution of products including coffee, sugar and spices. Our business connects producers, processors, exporters, roasters, distributors, cafés, hospitality businesses, food companies and institutional customers across international markets.
