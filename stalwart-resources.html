Origin Sourcing

Connecting Origins with Global Markets.

Stalwart Resources is the agricultural commodities, coffee, and food ingredients division of Stalwart Group.

We specialize in the sourcing, commercial trading, and regional distribution of high-demand commodities including coffee, sugar, and spices.

Our business connects plantation producers, industrial processors, exporters, roasters, distributors, cafés, hospitality businesses, food companies, and institutional customers across international markets. Through direct origin partnerships and commercial discipline, we deliver consistent grade specifications, competitive pricing, and supply stability.

Flagship Commodity

Coffee — Connecting Origins With Roasters & Cafés

Coffee forms a cornerstone of the Stalwart Resources portfolio. We operate across every tier of the specialty and commercial coffee industry:

Green Coffee Beans

Direct sourcing from verified estates and processing mills across premier Asian origins, with strict moisture, screen size, and defect grading.

Specialty & Micro-Lots

High-altitude, single-origin Arabica micro-lots featuring distinctive flavor profiles for boutique artisanal roasters and specialty coffee bars.

Commercial Green Coffee

High-volume Robusta and commercial Arabica supply for industrial roasters, instant coffee manufacturing, and institutional foodservice blends.

Roasted Coffee Solutions

Custom roast profiles packaged under private label or bulk for hospitality chains, boutique hotel groups, and café franchises.

Hospitality & Café Supply

Complete turnkey coffee procurement programs, scheduled deliveries, barista support, and freshness guarantees for restaurants and hotels.

Decaffeinated Coffee

Clean-water and Swiss-water processed decaffeinated lots offering natural flavor preservation for health-conscious commercial markets.

Agricultural Trade

Sugar Commercial Trading

Stalwart Resources participates in the commercial sourcing and international trade of refined and raw cane sugar for industrial, food processing, wholesale, and beverage manufacturing buyers.

  • Refined White Cane Sugar (ICUMSA 45 & Commercial Grades)
  • Bulk Containerized & Breakbulk Cargo Shipments
  • Food Industry & Beverage Manufacturing Contracts
Spice Heritage

Selected Spice Categories

Spices remain a foundational part of global culinary heritage and agricultural trade. We work across verified spice categories, connecting origin farmers with spice millers, food brands, and distributors.

  • Whole & Ground Spices for Industrial Food Processing
  • Direct Sourcing from India and Regional Spice Belts
  • Strict Lab Testing for Purity, Moisture, and Essential Oils
