Origin Sourcing

Connecting Origins with Global Markets.

Stalwart Resources is the agricultural commodities, coffee, and food ingredients division of Stalwart Group.

We specialize in the sourcing, commercial trading, and regional distribution of high-demand agricultural products including coffee, sugar, and selected spices.

Our business connects plantation producers, processors, exporters, roasters, distributors, cafés, hospitality businesses, food companies, and institutional customers across international markets. Through direct origin partnerships and commercial discipline, we deliver consistent grade specifications, competitive pricing, and supply stability.

Flagship Commodity

Coffee — Connecting Origins With Roasters & Cafés.

Coffee forms a cornerstone of the Stalwart Resources portfolio. We operate across every tier of the specialty and commercial coffee industry:

Green Coffee Beans

Direct sourcing from verified estates and processing mills across premier Asian origins, with strict moisture, screen size, and defect grading.

Specialty & Micro-Lots

High-altitude, single-origin Arabica micro-lots featuring distinctive flavor profiles for boutique artisanal roasters and specialty coffee bars.

Commercial Green Coffee

High-volume Robusta and commercial Arabica supply for industrial roasters, instant coffee manufacturing, and institutional foodservice blends.

Roasted Coffee Solutions

Custom roast profiles packaged under private label or bulk for hospitality chains, boutique hotel groups, and café franchises.

Hospitality & Café Supply

Complete turnkey coffee procurement programs, scheduled deliveries, barista support, and freshness guarantees for restaurants and hotels.

Decaffeinated Coffee

Clean-water and Swiss-water processed decaffeinated lots offering natural flavor preservation for health-conscious commercial markets.

Industrial Commodity

Sugar Commercial Trade.

Supplying food processing plants, beverage bottlers, confectioners, and institutional wholesalers with certified grades.

Stalwart Resources participates in the commercial sourcing and international trade of refined cane sugar and raw sugar. Our network connects high-capacity sugar mills in Thailand and India with buyers across international markets, focusing on grade consistency (ICUMSA 45), competitive pricing, and punctual maritime shipment.

ICUMSA 45 Refined Sugar

High-purity sparkling white cane sugar for confectionery and bottling.

Bulk Container Shipments

Containerized 50kg bag consignments and charter vessel bulk cargo.

Agricultural Heritage

Selected Spices Origin Sourcing.

Authentic Indian and regional whole and ground spices sourced directly from certified agricultural growers.

Spices remain an essential component of international agricultural trade and food culture. Stalwart Resources works across verified spice categories, connecting origin farmers with spice millers, food brands, and foodservice distributors. We enforce strict testing for moisture levels, essential oil content, and cleanliness.

  • Black Pepper, Cardamom, Cloves, Turmeric, Cumin, and Coriander
  • Direct Estate Relationships with Origin Farmers in South Asia
  • Phytosanitary Certification and Fumigation Guarantees for Export
